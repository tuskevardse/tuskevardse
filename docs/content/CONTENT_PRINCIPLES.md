# Content Principles

1. Start from visitor questions, not organizational self-description.
2. Every block should answer a real question, reduce uncertainty, help a decision, prove an important claim, give practical information, or provide a clear next action. Otherwise merge or delete it.
3. Show rather than declare: prefer observable situations to abstract claims about safety, community, attention or inclusion.
4. Optimize the complete visitor journey, not isolated sentences.
5. Shorten aggressively when two sections say the same thing.
6. Preserve verified facts; never fill gaps with plausible inventions. Mark uncertainty `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`.
7. Preserve stable `data-editor-*` identifiers; add stable English dot-separated identifiers to new editable content.
