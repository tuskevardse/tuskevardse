# Brand Voice

Voice: human, calm, inclusive, concrete, trustworthy, child-centered while treating parents as partners.

Do not sound elitist, over-marketed, psychologically superior, or promise guaranteed outcomes.

Central positioning hypothesis: **Nem kell eleve sportos gyereknek lenned ahhoz, hogy legyen helyed a sportban.** Do not repeat this mechanically; let the site demonstrate it.

Typical parent questions to answer quickly: Will my child enjoy it? What happens the first time? What if they are shy or unsure? What if they are not competitive? How do we choose a sport? Who can I ask? When and where are sessions?
