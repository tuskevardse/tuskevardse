# Anti-patterns

Watch for excessive repetition of: `kapcsolódás`, `közeg`, metaphorical `érkezés/megérkezés`, `élmény`, `fejlődési út/pálya`, `kapaszkodó`, `emberközpontú`, `biztonságos közeg`, `következő lépés`, `ritmus`.

These words are not banned; repetition is a warning sign.

Avoid repeated formulae such as title + three positive nouns + generic explanation, consecutive slogan sentences, repeated three-item abstract lists, and unsupported positive claims.

Never fabricate testimonials, coaches, statistics, awards, partnerships, schedules, locations, sports or results. Use cautious language for outcomes (`segíthet`, `jó irány lehet`) unless stronger claims are verified.
